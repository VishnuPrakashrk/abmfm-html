Engineering Systems Assessment

Core Advisory Service

We assess the condition, performance, capacity, reliability, and operational effectiveness of building engineering systems and critical infrastructure.

Our assessments can cover HVAC and cooling systems, electrical systems, mechanical systems, fire and life-safety systems, plumbing, water systems, BMS/controls, and other critical engineering infrastructure to identify deficiencies and investment basis.

We evaluate system condition and performance to determine remaining life, risks, and opportunities for optimization, ensuring safe, efficient, and resilient operations.
