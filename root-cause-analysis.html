Root Cause Analysis & Failure Investigation

Core Advisory Service

Recurring failures often indicate a deeper issue beyond immediate equipment fault. We provide structured Root Cause Analysis and failure investigation to identify why failures occur and what needs to change to prevent recurrence.

Our investigations address recurring HVAC and electrical failures, mechanical breakdowns, utility interruptions, water leakage, unplanned downtime, equipment underperformance, maintenance issues, contractor failures, operational incidents using 5 Whys, Fishbone, FMEA, Fault Tree.

We apply systematic RCA methodologies to move beyond symptoms and establish evidence-based corrective and preventive actions that enhance reliability.
