Aviation & Transit Infrastructure Solutions

From curb-to-gate operations to airside technical support, we maintain critical transit assets for high-volume public transport environments.

Terminal Operations

Janitorial automation, concourse climate management, passenger seating maintenance, and continuous high-touch zone hygiene protocols.

Baggage Systems

High-speed conveyor electrical maintenance, sortation drive inspection, automated bag drop mechanical upkeep, and downtime mitigation.

Fleet Maintenance

Ground Support Equipment (GSE) servicing, electric shuttle fleet charging infrastructure, maintenance, and vehicle lifecycle management.

Parking & Access

Automated license plate recognition (ALPR) systems, multi-story garage structure maintenance, EV charging stations, and shuttle logistics.

Airside Mechanical

Pre-conditioned air (PCA) units, 400Hz ground power units (GPU), jet bridge mechanical systems, and runway lighting electrical networks.

Safety & Compliance

FAA/TSA facility compliance audits, emergency backup generator maintenance, fire suppression testing, and perimeter security barrier O&M.
